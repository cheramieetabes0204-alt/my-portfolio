Transaction coordination

### Opening escrow on a double close in North Carolina

Google Workspace · DocuSign · PandaDoc

#### Problem

A double close puts two contracts on one property, each with its own price, deposit, and dates. Title needs all of it clearly laid out to open the file.

#### Action

I sent title one opening email with the documents attached and each contract's dates, prices, deposits, and parties set out separately. I also requested wiring instructions, escrow instructions, and the file number.

#### Result

Title had both contracts in one place from the first email. The resale was signed 29 days after the purchase contract, with settlement set 35 days ahead of the closing deadline.

### How I handle a deal

1. #### Read the file

   I review the CRM notes, the reported comps, and the market value in Podio or Follow Up Boss.
2. #### Research and build the package

   I research the property and create a due diligence report with the property details.
3. #### Call the buyers list

   I start with known buyers, presenting the deal by phone and text.
4. #### Skip trace new leads

   I pull neighbors, agents in the area, and landowners in the county.
5. #### Reach out to everyone

   I run outreach to all the leads gathered, by neighbor and agent campaign.
6. #### Follow up until there's an offer

   Interested parties go in the CRM. I list on the MLS and Facebook Marketplace, keep marketing offline, and stay on the first leads.

### Transaction coordination

- Open escrow and send title what they need to start.
- Track contract deadlines and follow up with title and escrow.
- Keep buyers, sellers, title, and my team updated.
- Prepare documents and route them for e-signature.
